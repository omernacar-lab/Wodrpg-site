// HOW IT WORKS

From WOD to XP in Seconds

01

Log Your WOD

Type or paste the workout of the day. Select the format — AMRAP, For Time, EMOM, or Strength — and enter your result.

02

AI Analyzes Everything

Our parser identifies every movement, calculates total volume, and scores your performance against expected benchmarks.

03

Earn XP & Level Up

XP is distributed across your four stats based on the workout composition. Deadlifts build Strength, runs boost Stamina, pull-ups grow Agility.

04

Battle & Compete

Your XP damages the weekly boss, updates the leaderboard, extends your streak, and unlocks achievements — all automatically.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 15, 2026

WodRPG ("we", "our", "us") operates the WodRPG mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: Email address used for authentication
  • Profile data: Display name, username, height, weight (optional)
  • Workout data: WOD descriptions, performance results, timestamps
  • Video data: PR verification videos stored securely in cloud storage

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the app experience
  • To calculate XP, stats, and leaderboard rankings
  • To verify PR submissions via AI analysis
  • To display your profile to other users in your box

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on Google Firebase servers in the Europe West region. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your workout data and profile information are only visible to other WodRPG users within the app as part of the leaderboard and box features.

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, and cloud storage
  • Google Gemini AI: WOD text analysis and PR video verification

Data Deletion

You can request complete deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at omer35nacar@icloud.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

WodRPG is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "last updated" date.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us at omer35nacar@icloud.com.
